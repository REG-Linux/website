## Projects this wiki draws on

**Batocera.linux.** REG Linux started from Batocera, and the system, port and engine
pages are based on the [Batocera wiki](https://wiki.batocera.org/), which is published
under CC0 1.0. The controller overlay images in `images/controller-overlays/` come from
the Batocera wiki as well.

**ROCKNIX.** Until November 2026 several handheld pages of this wiki contained text
taken from the [ROCKNIX documentation](https://github.com/ROCKNIX/rocknix.org)
(GPL-3.0) without credit. Those pages have been replaced by pages written for REG Linux
from its own sources. REG Linux also thanks the ROCKNIX project for its work on handheld
device support, which REG Linux builds on.

**ES-Theme-Canvas.** The system logos are taken from the ES-Theme-Canvas theme.

If you find text or images here that come from your project and are not credited, please
open an issue on [REG-Linux/website](https://github.com/REG-Linux/website/issues) and we
will fix it.
